        An interdisciplinary, international and interconfessional platform for the relationship between biblical studies and systematic theology, with a strict peer-review scheme for presentations and publications. Volumes appear with De Gruyter in the series Theologische Bibliothek Töpelmann, alongside special issues with the *International Journal of Systematic Theology* and further publications. The Forum has met annually at EuARe since 2019 and, since 2026, also at EABS. Proposals for volumes are welcome.

        [Visit the Forum website ↗](https://scriptureandtheology.org/)
